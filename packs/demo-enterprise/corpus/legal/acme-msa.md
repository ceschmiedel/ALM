# Master Services Agreement — Northwind Industries and Acme Cloud Services

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement" or "MSA") is entered into between
Northwind Industries B.V. ("Customer") and Acme Cloud Services GmbH ("Supplier").
The Agreement is governed by the laws of the Netherlands. The effective date is
1 March 2025 and the initial term is thirty-six (36) months.

## 3. Fees and Charges

The Customer shall pay the Supplier an annual platform fee of EUR 1,200,000,
invoiced quarterly in advance. Professional services are charged separately at
the rates set out in Schedule B. The annual platform fee is subject to an
increase of no more than 4% per contract year, effective on each anniversary of
the effective date.

## 4. Payment Terms

Payment terms are net 30 days from the date of a valid invoice. Late payment
accrues interest at 1% per month on the outstanding balance, calculated daily.
The Supplier may suspend service after sixty (60) days of non-payment, provided
it has given the Customer fifteen (15) days written notice of its intention to
suspend.

## 5. Service Levels

The Supplier warrants monthly availability of 99.9% measured over a calendar
month. Where availability falls below the warranted level, the Customer is
entitled to service credits calculated as follows: 5% of the monthly fee for
availability between 99.0% and 99.9%, 10% for availability between 98.0% and
99.0%, and 20% for availability below 98.0%. Service credits are the Customer's
sole and exclusive remedy for availability failures, and are capped at 20% of the
monthly fee in any given month.

## 6. Data Protection

The Supplier acts as processor in respect of Customer personal data and shall
process such data only on documented instructions from the Customer. The Supplier
shall notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 24 hours,
of becoming aware of a personal data breach. Sub-processors require the
Customer's prior written approval. Customer data shall be stored and processed
exclusively within the European Economic Area.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

7.1 Neither party excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury
caused by negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or for any
other liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

7.2 Subject to clause 7.1, the aggregate liability of each party under this
Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall
not exceed the total fees paid by the Customer in the twelve (12) months
immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

7.3 The cap in clause 7.2 does not apply to the Supplier's liability arising
from breach of clause 6 (Data Protection), breach of confidentiality, or the
Supplier's indemnity obligations under clause 8. In respect of breaches of
clause 6, the Supplier's aggregate liability shall not exceed twenty-four (24)
months of fees.

7.4 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit,
loss of anticipated savings, or loss of goodwill, howsoever arising.

## 8. Indemnities

The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against all losses arising from any
claim that the Services infringe a third party's intellectual property rights,
provided that the Customer notifies the Supplier promptly and permits the
Supplier to control the defence of the claim.

## 9. Term and Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach that remains
uncured thirty (30) days after written notice of the breach.

9.2 The Customer may terminate for convenience on ninety (90) days written
notice, subject to payment of an early termination charge equal to 25% of the
fees that would have been payable over the remainder of the then-current term.

9.3 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other party
becomes insolvent, enters administration, or ceases to carry on business.

9.4 On termination, the Supplier shall provide transition assistance for up to
six (6) months at the rates in Schedule B, and shall return or delete Customer
data at the Customer's election within thirty (30) days.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all information disclosed by the other that is
designated as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.
Confidentiality obligations survive termination for five (5) years.
