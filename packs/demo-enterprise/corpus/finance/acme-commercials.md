# Acme Engagement — Commercial Summary (Finance)

Internal finance note on the Northwind–Acme master services agreement. Prepared
by Commercial Finance. Figures are in EUR unless stated otherwise.

## Contract Value

The annual platform fee is EUR 1,200,000, invoiced quarterly in advance at
EUR 300,000 per quarter. Over the thirty-six month initial term the committed
platform spend is EUR 3,600,000 before uplift. The contractual uplift ceiling of
4% per contract year brings the maximum committed spend over the full term to
approximately EUR 3,746,000.

Professional services are charged separately and are not committed. Actual
professional services spend in the first twelve months was EUR 214,000.

## Payment Terms and Working Capital

Payment terms are net 30 days from a valid invoice. Because fees are billed
quarterly in advance, the working capital impact is a EUR 300,000 outflow at the
start of each quarter. Late payment carries interest of 1% per month, which is
an effective annual rate of approximately 12.7% when compounded — materially
above our cost of capital, so late payment is never economically rational here.

## Liability Cap — Finance Reading

The liability cap in clause 7.2 is expressed as the total fees paid in the twelve
months preceding the claim. At the current run rate that is EUR 1,200,000 of
platform fees. Finance's position is that the cap should be read on **fees
actually paid**, which includes professional services invoiced in that window.
On the last twelve months that basis gives EUR 1,414,000 rather than
EUR 1,200,000.

The carve-out in clause 7.3 for data protection breaches extends the cap to
twenty-four months of fees, which at the current run rate is EUR 2,400,000 on the
platform fee alone. This is the figure that should drive the insurance
assessment, not the twelve-month cap.

## Service Credits

Service credits are capped at 20% of the monthly fee, that is EUR 20,000 per
month against a monthly platform fee of EUR 100,000. Over a full year the
maximum recoverable through service credits is EUR 240,000. Credits are the sole
remedy for availability failures, so the recoverable amount does not scale with
the business impact of an outage.

## Early Termination

Terminating for convenience carries a charge of 25% of the fees remaining over
the then-current term. Terminating at the twenty-four month mark, with twelve
months remaining, would cost approximately EUR 300,000 in early termination
charges plus any transition assistance charged at Schedule B rates.

## Exposure Summary

Maximum contractual financial exposure, excluding the unlimited heads in clause
7.1: EUR 2,400,000 under the data protection carve-out. Standard exposure under
the general cap: EUR 1,200,000 on the platform-fee basis, or EUR 1,414,000 on the
fees-actually-paid basis.
